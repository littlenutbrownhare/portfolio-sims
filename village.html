Hi, I'm Mafy's hare

This little village is Mafy Hidalgo's portfolio. Every building is a page. Point me at one and I'll hop over and open it right here.

  • Click a building, or a dot on the minimap, and I'll walk there.
  • Each one opens a real page inside the panel.
  • Six acorns are hidden around the map. See if you can find them all.
  • It runs on Manila time, so it's night here when it's night for Mafy. Sound is on, tap the top corner to mute.
